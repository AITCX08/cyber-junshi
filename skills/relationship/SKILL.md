# Relationship Decisions

## Overview

Support relationship decisions without diagnosing, coercing, or bypassing consent. Treat the
other person's stated boundary as evidence, not as an obstacle to optimize around.

**REQUIRED SUB-SKILL:** Use cyber-junshi for the decision workflow and MCP tools.

## Relationship Rules

1. Call `assess_safety` first when the case mentions self-harm, violence, threats, coercion,
   stalking, blackmail, privacy exposure, or immediate danger. An emergency route ends message
   optimization and prioritizes immediate local help.
2. Put only observed communication and actions in `facts`. Put attachment styles, motives,
   personality labels, and predictions in `inferences`. Put missing context in `unknowns`.
3. Include waiting, no contact, or one respectful clarification among the options when relevant.
   Compare information gain and reversibility, not only the chance of getting a reply.
4. Make consent and boundaries stop conditions:
   - A clear request for no contact means stop contacting.
   - Blocking means do not use alternate accounts, friends, gifts, workplaces, or offline visits
     to regain access.
   - Silence does not prove motive and does not grant permission to escalate.
5. Keep financial, property, or safety logistics separate from emotional persuasion. Use a
   lawful, documented channel or qualified local professional when a real dispute exists.
6. Record an outcome only when the user explicitly asks.

## Message Shape

When contact is still appropriate, draft at most one voluntary, non-accusatory message with:

- the observed fact;
- one clear request;
- an easy way to decline;
- a commitment to respect the answer or non-answer.

Example:

> “这两天没收到你的回复。我想确认你是否还愿意继续沟通；如果不愿意，可以直接告诉我，我会尊重并停止追问。”

## Common Mistakes

- Treating “NPD”, “avoidant”, or another label as a diagnosis or danger signal.
- Optimizing for any reply instead of a voluntary, informative reply.
- Calling repeated contact “closure” after a boundary is clear.
- Presenting speculative motives as facts.
- Promising reconciliation or a guaranteed response.
